## What the app is

Elegant Ambient controls an aftermarket Bluetooth LE ambient lighting kit fitted to a car's
cabin. **It requires that hardware.** Without a compatible lighting controller powered and in
range, the app can be opened but has nothing to control.

The lighting kit is not made, sold or supported by the developer. The app is not affiliated
with, endorsed by, or connected to Mercedes-Benz or any vehicle manufacturer.

## Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock

Use of the app requires a subscription or the one-time lifetime unlock, after any free trial
period offered at purchase.

- Subscriptions **renew automatically** at the end of each period unless cancelled at least
  24 hours before it ends.
- The price, billing period and any free trial are shown in the app before you buy, in your own
  currency.
- Your account is charged for renewal within 24 hours of the end of the current period.
- If a free trial is offered and you buy a subscription before it ends, the unused part of the
  trial is forfeited.
- The **lifetime unlock is a single payment**. It does not renew and is not a subscription.

## Managing and cancelling

Subscriptions are managed in your store account, not in the app. On iOS: Settings → your name →
Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next
renewal; it does not refund the current period.

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies. The developer cannot issue
refunds for store purchases.

## Use in a vehicle

Do not operate the app while driving. Set the lighting before you move off, or use the voice
and CarPlay controls, which exist so the phone can stay where it is. You are responsible for
complying with the laws where you drive, including any rules about interior lighting colours
and their visibility from outside the vehicle.
