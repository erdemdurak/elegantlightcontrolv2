What the app is

Elegant Ambient controls an aftermarket Bluetooth LE ambient lighting kit fitted to a car's cabin. It requires that hardware. Without a compatible lighting controller powered and in range, the app can be opened but has nothing to control.

The lighting kit is not made, sold or supported by the developer. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Mercedes-Benz or any vehicle manufacturer.

Use of the app requires a subscription or the one-time lifetime unlock, after any free trial period offered at purchase.

  • The price, billing period and any free trial are shown in the app before you buy, in your own currency.

  • The lifetime unlock is a single payment. It does not renew and is not a subscription.

Managing and cancelling

Subscriptions are managed in your store account, not in the app. On iOS: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Payments and subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the current period.

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their own policies. The developer cannot issue refunds for store purchases.

Use in a vehicle

Do not operate the app while driving. Set the lighting before you move off, or use the voice and CarPlay controls, which exist so the phone can stay where it is.
