Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Elegant Ambient controls an aftermarket Bluetooth LE ambient lighting kit installed in a car. This policy describes what the app does and does not do with information.

No account, no server

The app has no sign-in and no user account. There is no server operated by the developer, and nothing you do in the app is transmitted to the developer. There is no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no advertising.

What stays on your device

Your colours, presets, brightness levels, schedule, saved palette and the identifier of the last lighting controller you connected to are stored on the device only, by the operating system's normal app storage. Deleting the app removes them.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used for one purpose: to find and talk to your lighting controller. The app acts only as a Bluetooth central, sending colour and brightness commands. It does not use Bluetooth for location, presence detection, or to identify you, and it requests no location permission. Scan results stay on the device.

Purchases

Access to the app is sold through the App Store and Google Play. When you buy or restore a subscription or the lifetime unlock, the app asks Apple or Google whether this account owns it, and stores the yes-or-no answer on the device.

Payments are handled entirely by Apple and Google. The developer never sees or receives your card details, billing address, or any other payment information. These store requests are the only network traffic the app makes; Apple's and Google's own privacy policies govern them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing that could identify anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted here with a new date.
