Elegant Ambient

Cabin lighting control for an aftermarket Bluetooth LE kit

Two independently addressable zones — the door trim lines and centre console, and the air vents and tweeter grilles — with presets built for a dark leather interior, a daily schedule, automatic preset cycling, Siri phrases and a CarPlay screen.

The app requires a compatible Bluetooth LE lighting controller installed in the vehicle. It does nothing on its own.
